# Showcase Prompts — Period Close Status

These prompts are adapted for this skill from common financial-analysis patterns in Oracle NetSuite documentation and are intentionally company-neutral.

1. Summarize the status of the current financial close and identify the remaining blockers in dependency order.
2. Identify unposted, unapproved, or otherwise incomplete financial activity that may prevent [period] from closing.
3. Show which close tasks can proceed now and which tasks depend on unresolved upstream work.
4. Compare the current close progress with the prior close cycle and identify areas taking longer than usual.
5. Identify material reconciliation, journal, AP, AR, or other finance exceptions that could affect the target close date.
6. Summarize close readiness by subsidiary and highlight any subsidiary that is materially behind the broader close.
7. Identify late financial postings or unusual activity in the closing period that may need controller review.
8. Prepare a concise close-status report with completed work, open work, blockers, owners, and next dependency.
9. Assess whether the available evidence supports locking [period] by [target date] and explain any conditions that still need to be met.
10. List the next five highest-priority close actions based on dependency and financial impact rather than a flat checklist.
