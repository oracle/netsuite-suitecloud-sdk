# Executive Financial Reporting changelog

## Included in SuiteSkills 1.0.0

Component version: 1.0.1. Source version: 1.0.1.

Before failing the cash tie-out, align the cash-flow and balance-sheet definitions, book, entity, period and currency. Separately identify evidenced Undeposited Funds and other included cash components; reconcile the complete bridge without plugging an unexplained difference. Only the unexplained residual constitutes a tie-out failure. Flag consolidation/elimination coverage and open-period preliminary status.
