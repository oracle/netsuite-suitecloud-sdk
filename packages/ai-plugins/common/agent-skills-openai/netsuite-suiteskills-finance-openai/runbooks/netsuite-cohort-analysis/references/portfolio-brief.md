# CFO response formats

Use this package-local reference for every answer. Match the CFO's question before choosing a format; do not make a focused question carry an unrequested portfolio table. Every cohort row remains a verified customer-acquisition month; product, plan, pricing model, segment, and renewal window are cuts within it. Keep full analytic coverage internally even when displaying only top rows.

## Choose the smallest complete response

- **Broad portfolio or cohort-ranking question:** Usually fit within three-quarters of a page. Lead with the answer, a compact scope/source and coverage line, the comparable beginning-base-weighted and median GRR/NRR evidence when relevant, observed gross loss versus actual expansion, and a ranked Markdown table of at most three or four verified acquisition months with CFO decision and delegated owner. Label it “Top N of M assessable acquisition months,” state material omissions, and do not create a ranked table when no cohort is assessable. Two months show a change, not persistence.
- **Focused signal or bounded what-if:** Lead with the directly requested finding or scenario, the relevant cohort/baseline, recurring-dollar effect, one CFO decision or owner, and the decisive limitation. A table and a 250–350-word portfolio brief are not required. If rates or a forecast are unavailable, say so without padding with unrelated metrics.
- **Explicitly comprehensive question or material exception:** Show all requested assessable rows, history, or exceptions even when longer than the usual brief. Expand only the components that answer the question; do not hide a material membership or reconciliation failure to fit a length target.

Use Markdown tables only when they clarify a comparison and give each column its own header, separator, and data cell. Keep open renewal exposure out of a retrospective answer unless a forward window was requested; do not end such an answer by proposing one.

## Expanded detail when requested or needed

Use only components relevant to the request; one detailed question does not require every section below.

1. **Scope:** Decision question; analysis/comparison periods; renewal window only if requested; verified acquisition-month definition and customer count; optional product/plan/segment filters; organizational scope; currency; MRR/ARR and Current/Committed basis; data-through date.
2. **Monthly trend:** For each completed month show beginning-base-weighted and median cohort GRR/NRR, eligible cohort count, beginning base, gross loss, and actual expansion. For a month-over-month behavioral claim, show matched-cohort count, paired weighted change, number declining, and exclusions/newly eligible cohorts. A large weighted decline with steady median suggests concentration only if customer-level distribution confirms it; two months do not establish persistence.
3. **Explicitly requested monthly cohort grid:** Rows are verified acquisition months, columns completed calendar months or clearly labeled months-since-acquisition. Each valid cell shows `GRR / NRR` in that order. If direction helps, use plain `↑`, `↓`, `→` beside the numeric percentage-point change for each rate; `—` means unavailable, not zero, and a baseline has no direction. Disclose rounding tolerance and missing cells. Split wide views into readable tables. Never relabel native acquisition-baseline MRR or previous-month logo-retention visualizations as GRR/NRR. Do not generate this grid by default.
4. **Complete assessed-cohort table:** When requested or required to avoid hiding a material exception, show every identity-verified assessable acquisition month. Include comparable prior/current GRR/NRR, starting base, observed churn/downsell and expansion, concentration, and CFO decision with owner. Add logo retention/count when relevant and validated active renewal ARR/customer count only for a requested forward window. Separate actual loss, open exposure, and upside. Rates with zero base, failed bridge, or unresolved pre-acquisition actual activity are unavailable, not 0%; recompute on verified members or withhold the affected cohort. Never silently truncate the complete table or footnote a material membership conflict below a ranked row. Product/plan/segment details are within-cohort breakdowns, not substitute rows.
5. **Headline and bridge:** Keep gross loss and actual expansion separate; add validated open renewal exposure only when requested. If useful, bridge beginning recurring amount, churn, downsell, expansion, and ending amount on the same starting base, excluding New and requiring reconciliation.
6. **Optional renewal grid:** For a requested forward window, rows are acquisition months and columns renewal months or quarters, with validated open recurring exposure and customer counts. Construct from active renewals, not the acquisition-cohort retention visual. Keep candidate, stale, or duplicate-looking terms outside ranked exposure; do not color by assumed churn probability.
7. **Decision and coverage:** In a broad portfolio view, highlight up to three or four CFO decisions, delegated owners, and success measures without hiding other assessed cohorts. Weigh gross loss, rate change, breadth, persistence, concentration, feasibility, evidence, and requested forward exposure. For a focused what-if, show only the relevant baseline metric, percentage-point/relative assumption, bounded incremental ARR, and what would be required to call it forecast impact. For renewal, count active terms, metric matches, and unresolved terms by source mechanism; inaccessible mechanisms make the view partial.
8. **Footer:** Metric source, coverage, unreconciled gaps, material assumptions, and approved-forecast status only where relevant.

## Markdown rendering and language

Use Markdown headings, valid tables, and short text bridges only—no HTML, CSS, SVG, Mermaid, generated image, or chart renderer. Values must be understandable without arrows or color. Do not show placeholder charts, fabricated values, or an unlabeled partial-membership grid. Call churn/downsell **recurring revenue lost**, upcoming renewal amount **exposure**, verified added expansion **actual**, and a documented possible add **opportunity**. A proposed mitigation protects an unknown portion of exposure without an approved recoverability model.
