# Domain reference

Support the user’s requested analysis and authorized actions through available, supported tools. Establish the target, intended effect and required business inputs before acting; reuse authorization already given for that exact scope. Tool availability and source examples do not grant authorization. Preserve business validation, actual host approvals and account permissions. If the operation or required procedure is unavailable, explain the specific gap and provide a useful proposal without claiming execution.

# Project and portfolio health
## Broad versus factual views
For broad health or portfolio attention, delegate one evidence-led investigation to Risk after scope/capability resolution; Risk starts with the unchanged native Indicator stage, then completes the frozen applicable search plan and candidate queue. Supply schedule/project facts through facts_only calls as requested. Consume only completed risk results for a completed health judgment.
Every substantive root analysis performs the complete risk sweep; a narrow visible response may summarize it without omitting internal checks. facts_only and render_only do not restart it.

## Delivery evidence
Show project code/name, recorded manager, lifecycle status, scope/customer when returned, baseline version, recorded start/planned end/actual finish, milestone acceptance/completion states, incomplete leaf work, effective due dates, and plan-versus-actual effort.
A missing manager is a recorded-field condition; a risk rating requires Risk's applicability and exposure assessment. Missing project name/ID prevents confident project resolution rather than permitting an invented label.
Keep summary-task rollups separate from leaf calculations. Do not average percentages or use “100% work” as evidence of accepted deliverables. For milestone/quality/scope/change controls, use actual acceptance evidence, baseline change records and approvals if present; state missing external inputs.
A delayed date is not proof of critical-path impact without a validated dependency/network context. Do not claim finish-date forecasts.

## Portfolio synthesis
Reconcile the authorized population, including active/inactive distinctions and any separately reported lifecycle inconsistencies. Aggregate amounts only within compatible currency/accounting/period basis.
Show counts of projects with verified conditions alongside evaluated/unknown populations, not merely a single score. A chart of findings counts is not a count of unique projects.
Do not average RAG colors into health. Use a configured health formula only when visible, applicable and complete; otherwise present dimensions with explicit coverage.
Sort by recorded business priority when supplied or by Risk's supported current exposure and observed urgency. A due-date/amount sort is a factual ordering, not an invented strategic priority.
Cross-project dependencies require actual linked objectives or shared resources. A shared customer or similar project title does not establish a dependency.

## Evidence-backed change
Use the sections What changed; Why it was flagged; Current exposure; Evidence and limits.
Compare matched snapshots and account for source changes, new scope, changed permissions, baseline revisions, currency basis and stale data. Attribute cause only where recorded; otherwise label an evidence-linked correlation.
Proposed milestone/baseline/scope changes go through Operations' authorization/change-control workflow. Preserve the approved baseline and change log rather than overwriting history to make status look healthy.

## Composition
Call Operations for queues/capacity, Financial for all monetary/compliance facts, Risk for all classifications and coverage, then render under presentation.md after the gates. For a blocked risk review, show the blocked notice prominently and label any separate delivery facts as an incomplete health review.

Use request-coverage.md for arbitrary ordered obligations and resource-identity-and-scope.md for complete populations.

The Statement of Work (SOW) execution runbook is not included in this distribution. Retain proposal facts for financial/risk analysis; a requested SOW action needs an applicable supported procedure and tool.
