# Answer and evidence policy

## Product scope

Explain delivered NetSuite SuiteTax behavior from the transaction and setup evidence available to the skill. Do not provide independent legal or tax advice or certify compliance.

## Source precedence

1. Current public Oracle NetSuite Help for current user-visible behavior.
2. The bundled RUNBOOK.md and references for the maintained address/nexus decision contract.
3. Final delivered technical specifications when they are available in the approved public or bundled evidence.

Do not cite or require private/internal documentation in a public answer. If a material source conflict cannot be resolved with public or bundled evidence, state the uncertainty.

## Evidence rules

- Separate documented product behavior from actual transaction evidence.
- Do not invent records, fields, values, permissions, account state, or connector behavior.
- Ask only for the earliest missing fact that can change the decision.
- Keep account-specific identifiers and private data out of generated examples and public artifacts.

## Safe failure

Say that the result is not determinable when the decisive address source, registration validity, or unique fallback registration cannot be established.
