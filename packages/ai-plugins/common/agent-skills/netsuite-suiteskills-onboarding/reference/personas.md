# Role and goal examples

Personas are optional presentation defaults. The user's work and goal determine relevance; permission breadth never determines competence, seniority or consent. An Executive is a whole-business remit the user actually describes, not an inferred title. Executive and NetSuite Administrator remain separate role choices. Correct a mismatch immediately.

Question 2 stays open. Show up to three examples in plain text, then preserve the user's own goal verbatim. The following routes are illustrative candidates from the selected reference catalog; check actual session availability, descriptions and scope before offering them. A catalog name alone proves no connector capability or account access.

| Role / persona | Example goals | Candidate workflows, only when exposed and suitable |
|---|---|---|
| Finance and accounting / Closer | Close with fewer surprises; understand unusual movements; prioritize supported bank reconciliation review | Period Close Status; Variance and Flux Analysis; Financial Statement Review; Bank Reconciliation Prioritization |
| Collections / Cash Collector | Prioritize overdue balances; understand a customer's open invoices | Invoice Collections; Credit Management; supported receivables reports |
| Purchasing / Spend Steward | Understand upcoming bills; explain vendor spend changes | Payment Prioritization; Vendor Spend Analysis; Vendor Bill Processing Time |
| Operations and supply chain / Fulfillment Planner | Find late orders; understand stock shortages; see inbound supply | Fulfillment performance insights; Inventory Availability Lookup; Inventory Exceptions Review; suitable supported reports |
| Sales and customer-facing / Revenue Driver | Understand customer orders; explore pipeline when supported | Customer Service Order Triage; current supported sales reports when actually available |
| Executive | Understand results and cash; know what deserves attention | Executive financial reporting; Cash and Liquidity; supported financial review workflows |
| NetSuite Administrator | Understand access; identify available reports and customizations | A currently exposed administration specialist or narrowly supported connector metadata. The reference catalog does not supply a dedicated user-audit specialist; do not invent one. |
| Other | State the intended result in your own words | Match current descriptions and verify required procedure; ask one focused question if ambiguous |

Map by business outcome, time horizon and decision, not keywords alone. A missing specialist is a capability gap, not a reason to invent a replacement or claim another workflow certifies the same outcome. In particular, an intercompany balance review does not establish consolidation tie-out or close readiness, and another finance skill does not satisfy Data Visualization's actual Finance Analyst prerequisite.

## Framing and defaults

- Close/reporting: periods and scopes stated; exceptions with evidence, estimates labeled.
- Collections/payables: as-of date, currency and relevant entity; no implied scheduling or payment execution.
- Fulfillment: actual order/location scope and item evidence; avoid promising stock coverage from a partial read.
- Executive: fewer numbers and clear implications; preserve subsidiary, book and currency basis. Consolidated is only a proposed scope until supported.
- Administrator/inherited account: one sentence of orientation; distinguish observed configuration, denied access and unknown capability.

No date, accounting period, subsidiary or permission is manufactured from a persona. The selected specialist resolves business scope and required sources. If the first workflow cannot run, offer another relevant choice and wait for selection; an empty successful result remains a valid answer.
