# Finance and Accounting

Support the requested analysis and source-supported authorized actions through available tools. Individual analytical procedures do not gain unspecified write operations. Preserve account permissions, business validation and result verification.

Select from [index](index.json). Apply [shared guidance](shared/README.md).

## Runbooks

- [Expense Close Assistant](runbooks/netsuite-expense-close-assistant/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-002`: Review NetSuite expense-report exceptions before close, show an interactive exception dashboard, and prepare owner-directed follow-ups. Supports communication drafts or explicitly authorized messages; excludes expense-record changes and period closure.
- [Match Vendor Credits to Bills](runbooks/netsuite-match-vendor-credits-to-bills/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-003`: Find unused vendor credits and propose allocations to compatible open bills using verified remaining balances, vendor, subsidiary, currency and accounts payable account. Show oldest-eligible-bill matches, residuals and unverified pairs. Read-only; does not apply credits or prioritize unrelated payments.
- [Cash and Liquidity](runbooks/netsuite-cash-and-liquidity/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-006`: Assess cash position, accessible versus restricted cash, near-term funding pressure and runway for a specified entity and period. Use for liquidity and payroll-coverage questions; state verified inputs and scenario assumptions. The formal cash flow statement and invoice collections belong to their respective workflows.
- [Executive Financial Reporting](runbooks/netsuite-executive-finance-reporting/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-007`: Use to assemble an executive or board finance package from named upstream finance-skill results. Preserve metric provenance and tie-outs, surface incomplete evidence, and prepare a scoped narrative and proposed actions; route missing analysis to its owning skill.
- [Finance Controls Review](runbooks/netsuite-finance-controls-review/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-008`: Use to review finance control exceptions across periods, document evidence coverage and recurrence, and propose remediation. Distinguish isolated observations from potential deficiencies; the control owner makes the deficiency determination.
- [Financial Statement Review](runbooks/netsuite-financial-statement-review/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-009`: Use to retrieve and interpret an income statement, balance sheet, or cash flow statement for a resolved period, entity, currency and accounting book. Reconcile statement relationships, calculate supported financial indicators, and label open-period or incomplete results; route driver analysis and board-package assembly to their owning skills.
- [Invoice Collections](runbooks/netsuite-invoice-collections/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-011`: Build an evidence-based collections queue for open receivables, separating contact blockers before ranking verified recovery factors. Use for invoice follow-up priorities and outreach drafts; sending messages or creating follow-up tasks requires the specific authorized scope and a supported capability. General ledger reconciliation and standalone days-sales-outstanding trends belong to other workflows.
- [Journal Entry Review](runbooks/netsuite-journal-entry-review/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-012`: Use to screen posted and unposted journals for evidenced review criteria, explain each flagged entry and report assessment coverage. Prepare requested correcting-entry drafts; post only through a supported authorized workflow after validating the exact entry and accounting impact.
- [Period Close Status](runbooks/netsuite-period-close-status/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-013`: Use when asked where the close stands, what is blocking period close, or whether the target lock date can be met. Review observed accounting-period states, close-task evidence, owners, dependencies and pending financial activity. Keep missing evidence and specialist coverage explicit. Read-only; does not execute close tasks or lock a period.
- [Variance and Flux Analysis](runbooks/netsuite-variance-analysis/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-015`: Use to explain material differences from a verified budget, forecast, prior period or prior year. Reconcile evidence-backed drivers to the total variance, distinguish recurring and timing effects, and disclose unexplained residuals; do not silently substitute a missing comparison basis.
- [Payment Prioritization](runbooks/netsuite-payment-prioritization/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-016`: Build read-only NetSuite payment proposals across Payment Run-eligible A/P with compatible vendor-credit and negative bill-payment or journal offsets. Prioritize by due dates, payee importance, discounts and cash constraints; explain selections and follow-ups. Use AP analytics for timing-only questions.
- [Vendor Bill Processing Time](runbooks/netsuite-vendor-bill-processing-time/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-017`: Analyze NetSuite accounts-payable vendor bill lifecycle performance through the available MCP connector: days-to-pay including prepayment applications, first cash payment versus final settlement, approval duration, entry lag, payment-terms compliance, open-bill aging, trends and outliers. Use for AP processing-time, payment-speed, slow-vendor, or bill lifecycle questions and dashboards, including “invoice” when the context is AP. Read-only analytics; does not create or approve payments. Use payment prioritization when the user wants to decide which bills to pay next.
- [Vendor Spend Analysis](runbooks/netsuite-vendor-spend-analysis/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-018`: Ranks vendors by spend or current open balance and shows each vendor's share of the total, recent spend movement, aging, and other concentration signals in an in-chat bar chart with linked records. Use to identify top or biggest vendors, see where accounts payable (AP) spend is concentrated, compare vendor spend for a period, find vendors above an amount, or answer who the business owes the most. Read-only; use for vendor-level ranking and concentration review, not bill-level processing-time analysis or decisions about which bills to pay.
- [Labor Cost Advisor](runbooks/netsuite-labor-cost-advisor/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-019`: Build a payroll-backed labor-cost dashboard when Payroll, both labor reports, and Labor Costs Setup are accessible; otherwise provide the bundled illustrative dashboard preview. Use for labor-cost what-if scenarios.
- [CFO Cohort Analysis](runbooks/netsuite-cohort-analysis/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-020`: Analyze customer acquisition-month retention and annual recurring revenue (ARR) exposure for a chief financial officer (CFO). Use for gross revenue retention (GRR), net revenue retention (NRR), logo retention, churn, downsell, within-cohort product or segment cuts, renewal exposure and bounded what-ifs. Excludes customer renewal books, billing queues, forecast creation and commercial changes.
- [Revenue Leakage](runbooks/netsuite-revenue-leakage/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-021`: Review potential invoicing shortfalls, credits requiring review and Advanced Revenue Management (ARM) plan or element exceptions as of a date. Use for underbilling, unbilled delivery, recorded subscription or schedule gaps, held plans and missing elements. Reports separate billing and accounting findings with coverage; excludes recurring-retention dashboards, transaction correction, collections and revenue-recognition certification.
- [Intercompany Balance Explorer](runbooks/netsuite-intercompany-balance-explorer/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-022`: Review intercompany exposure, subsidiary-pair differences and journal activity for a date range or as-of date. Use for read-only intercompany investigation with verified non-overlapping subsidiaries and comparable currency, book and posting bases; posting, approval and elimination actions require a separate workflow.
- [SuiteTax Nexus Explainer](runbooks/netsuite-suitetax-nexus-explainer/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-023`: Explain automatic SuiteTax nexus selection, fallback or failure for sales and purchases, using transaction addresses, registration validity and override evidence. Use for general rules or a specific transaction; distinguish business-to-business and business-to-consumer treatment. Tax amounts, legal advice and record changes are outside scope.
- [Overdue Invoice Change Review](runbooks/netsuite-overdue-invoice-change-review/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-024`: Investigate payments, credits and audited amount edits affecting current overdue invoices or a selected invoice. Use to explain observed receivable changes with evidence and an explicitly qualified bridge when a prior amount is supplied. Historical aging reconstruction, collections outreach and record changes are outside scope.
- [Project Financial Assistant](runbooks/netsuite-project-financial-assistant/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-025`: Review project revenue, observed cost, profitability, unbilled balances and aging from authorized NetSuite evidence. Use for project financial exceptions; produce a local HTML dashboard by default or honor an explicit text/data format. Read-only; budget-versus-actual comparison is unavailable in this renderer.
- [Bank reconciliation prioritization](runbooks/netsuite-bank-reconciliation-prioritization/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-026`: Assess and prioritize native bank and credit-card reconciliation work: matching queues, suggestions, differences and supported close-readiness conclusions. Read-only; excludes account changes, enterprise performance management reconciliation and payment-processor settlement.
- [Credit Management](runbooks/netsuite-credit-management/RUNBOOK.md) — `RB-FIN-027`: Review today's customer credit exposure, limit exceptions and signed overdue aging. Use for current customer or portfolio credit reviews; default undated requests to all role-accessible customer records today. Historical comparisons, collections outreach and record changes are outside scope.
