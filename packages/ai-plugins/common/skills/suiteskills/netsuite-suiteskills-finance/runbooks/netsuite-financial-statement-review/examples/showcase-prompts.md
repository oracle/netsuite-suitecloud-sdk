# Showcase Prompts — Financial Statement Review

These prompts are adapted for this skill from common financial-analysis patterns in Oracle NetSuite documentation and are intentionally company-neutral.

1. Analyze the Income Statement for [period] in [subsidiary] and highlight the financial metrics and line items that require attention.
2. Review gross margin, operating margin, and net margin for [period] and explain the main drivers behind the change in profitability.
3. Compare the Income Statement for [period] with the same period last year and identify the three largest favorable and unfavorable changes.
4. Analyze revenue growth and expense growth across [period range] and identify any trend where costs are growing faster than revenue.
5. Review the Balance Sheet as of [date], confirm that assets equal liabilities plus equity, and calculate the current ratio.
6. Identify any Balance Sheet account that represents a material concentration within its category and explain why it deserves attention.
7. Review the Cash Flow Statement for [period] and summarize operating, investing, and financing cash flows.
8. Explain the main non-cash items affecting the difference between reported profit and operating cash generation for [period].
9. Create a concise executive financial overview for [period] covering revenue, profit, margins, cash flow, and balance-sheet health.
10. Identify three financial strengths and three financial risks visible in the latest closed-period statements and show the NetSuite evidence behind each.
