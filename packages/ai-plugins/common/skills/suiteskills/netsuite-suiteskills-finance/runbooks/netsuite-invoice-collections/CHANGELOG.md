# Invoice Collections changelog

## Included in SuiteSkills 1.0.0

Component version: 1.0.1. Source version: 1.0.1.

Build an evidence-based collections queue for open receivables, separating contact blockers before ranking verified recovery factors. Use for invoice follow-up priorities and outreach drafts; sending messages or creating follow-up tasks requires the specific authorized scope and a supported capability. General ledger reconciliation and standalone days-sales-outstanding trends belong to other workflows.

Use metadata-first remaining-balance logic, payment-link coverage measurement, exclusion of unsupported payment features rather than zero-scoring, unattributed AR and per-item ranking confidence.
