# Showcase Prompts - Finance controls review

1. Review finance controls for [period range] and show a coverage matrix separating verified checks from evidence that is unavailable.
2. Identify recurring reconciliation or journal exceptions across the periods that are accessible and state the confidence impact if fewer periods are available.
3. Review suspense and clearing balances at period end, validating report sign orientation before treating detail rows as exceptions.
4. Assess access, journal support, reconciliation aging, transaction coding, and close discipline without treating missing evidence as a pass.
5. Prepare remediation recommendations for the verified control observations and distinguish observations from control-owner deficiency determinations.
