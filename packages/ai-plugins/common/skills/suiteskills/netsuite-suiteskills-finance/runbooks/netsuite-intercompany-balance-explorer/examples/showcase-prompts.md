# Showcase prompts

Use these prompts one at a time. They are read-only and should return a single text-first Markdown review.

1. **Largest pair differences**

   > Review intercompany balances for July through September 2026 and identify the largest verified subsidiary-pair mismatches and any candidate differences separately. Resolve the subsidiary hierarchy first and do not use parent, consolidated, or roll-up balances alongside descendants.

2. **Receivable/payable exposure**

   > For the current accounting month through today, compare the intercompany receivable/payable exposure by subsidiary and explain any compatible pair differences. Resolve the subsidiary hierarchy first; report only non-overlapping analysis units, do not convert currencies, and exclude future-dated activity.

3. **Journal activity**

   > Investigate intercompany journal activity for US - Sub 1 and US - Sub 2 from August 1 through August 31, 2026, including any pending-approval journals. Keep the review read-only, keep pending journals separate from posted tie-outs, and use bounded journal detail only after the applicable statement evidence.

4. **Specific pair tie-out**

   > Explain whether the intercompany balances for US - Sub 1 and US - Sub 2 tie as of September 30, 2026. Resolve the subsidiary hierarchy first, exclude any parent or roll-up entity from the pair calculation, and state the evidence supporting the conclusion. If mapping or counterparty evidence is incomplete, report a candidate tie or candidate difference rather than a confirmed verdict.

5. **Reconciliation and elimination follow-up**

   > Review intercompany reconciliation and elimination results for July through September 2026, compare them with the financial statements for the verified active non-overlapping subsidiaries, and identify what needs human follow-up. Keep reconciliation, elimination, posted journal, and pending-approval populations separate.
