# Showcase Prompts — Journal entry review

These prompts are adapted for this skill from common financial-analysis patterns in Oracle NetSuite documentation and are intentionally company-neutral.

1. Review manual journal entries posted in [period] and identify entries that warrant additional controller review.
2. Find large or unusual journal entries in [period] and explain the specific reason each one was flagged.
3. Identify round-dollar journal entries that are unusual for the accounts or departments used.
4. Review journal entries posted outside normal business patterns and show the evidence behind any anomaly.
5. Find journals involving account and department combinations that are rare compared with historical posting patterns.
6. Review accrual journals for reversal evidence and identify entries where the expected reversal is missing or unclear.
7. Identify journals posted to suspense, clearing, or other temporary accounts and summarize the offsetting entries.
8. Review selected journals for approval status, preparer-approver separation, period status, and dimensional coding.
9. Compare journal activity in [period] with prior periods and identify material changes in frequency, amount, or posting patterns.
10. Create a prioritized journal-review list for [period] with amount, posting context, reason for review, and next safe step.
