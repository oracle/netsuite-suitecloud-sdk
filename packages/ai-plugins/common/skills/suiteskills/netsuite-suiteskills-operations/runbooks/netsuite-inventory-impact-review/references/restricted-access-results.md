# Restricted or unavailable results

Answer from visible evidence only. Do not bypass permissions or infer invisible records.

- A failed identity lookup leaves identity unresolved; do not classify it. Explain the lookup limitation and ask for a useful identifying detail only when that could help.
- After identity verification, NO_ACCESSIBLE_MATCHING_DATA means no matching accessible line evidence; IMPACT_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE means no true flag and at least one unavailable flag. Both yield Not confirmed.
- Classification errors, invalid output and partial negative evidence yield Not confirmed, not None. Do not repeat classification, fetch details or query another source to manufacture a conclusion.
- If the user supplied an internal ID, identity verification still occurs before classification. Once classification fails or is unavailable, do not make further existence, display-field, detail or product-guidance reads for that reference.
- Details failure after Confirmed retains the status and classification count, with the missing-details statement.

Use only the cause actually reported. Do not diagnose permissions, missing features or absent records from an unexplained empty result. Continue independent references and preserve their results.
