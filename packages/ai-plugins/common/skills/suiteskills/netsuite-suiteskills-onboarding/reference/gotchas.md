# Common mistakes

- Fresh user: ask role, then open goal. Do not begin with an account scan or a forced menu. Skip and a directly stated task are valid paths.
- Returning user: establish intended ownership/context before quoting saved content. Matching subsidiary lists or connector labels do not prove identity. Historical findings require current validation.
- Plan: offer actual exposed workflows and readable runbooks, not names copied from an old catalog. A packaged skill may not be loaded; a loaded skill may lack account access.
- Controls: use a real supported submission event, or chat text. Copy/open/render is not execution. A preselected option is not submitted consent.
- Error: distinguish denial, empty success, partial coverage and unavailable source. Offer another workflow; do not run it merely because the first failed.
- Scope/action: reuse exact scope and authorization already established. A role or goal is not authority. Keep the specialist's business checks, inspect actual tool effects and reconcile ambiguous writes before retrying.
- Continuity: preview the exact local snapshot/destination and respect declined persistence. File creation, readback, future discovery and Project application are different states. Never claim automatic cross-session continuity.
- Visual: no invented pre-discovery entities, currency, hierarchy or successful account state. Geographic grouping is not legal hierarchy, accounting consolidation or eliminated intercompany balances.
- Vocabulary: use plain business labels; explain abbreviations before using them in independent outputs. Avoid claiming the AI never guesses or that all hosts work without evidence.
