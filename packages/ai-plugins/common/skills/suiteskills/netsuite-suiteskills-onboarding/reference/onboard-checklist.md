# Discovery and fallback

## Minimal discovery

For role/goal orientation, exposed skill descriptions and readable category indexes are normally enough. Discovery is optional and tied to a current need, not a mandatory account scan. Preserve the requested account/connector; if several are exposed and the intended one is unclear for account work, resolve that ambiguity rather than probing all accounts.

Use current supported session/account metadata only when needed. Inspect callable schemas, preserve exposed identity fields and evidence sources, and do not assume any particular account-ID field or table exists. No account query is needed to give a short text greeting. Do not collect transaction/customer/vendor/employee populations merely for personalization.

For selected business work, follow the specialist's domain retrieval path and shared safeguards. Current metadata, bounded retrieval, output meaning and effective access remain the specialist's responsibility. Do not route around a denial or a missing schema by guessing a different query. Permission denial, inaccessible metadata, empty data and transient error remain distinct. Subsidiary names/counts and legal names do not prove unique account/user identity.

## Fallback areas

Only after Skip, no usable goal, two unsuccessful adjustments, or a request for broad choices, show a short list: Finance and close; Receivables and collections; Purchasing and payables; Inventory and fulfillment; Sales and customers; Executive overview; NetSuite administration; Something else. Executive and Administrator stay separate. Adapt the count to supported host controls or use text. No menu is mandatory for a specific business request.

After a selection, offer two to four relevant supported jobs from the starter prompts. Omit workflows proven unavailable; keep uncertain capability labeled as uncertain. Never interpret an unknown requirement as absence or zero. No role access probe establishes the user's job or competence.

## Preferences

Ask optional presentation preferences only when requested or at a natural end. A preference for daily/weekly review expresses cadence; it does not schedule anything. Unsupported formats remain unavailable; don't add services or promise arbitrary exports. Keep goal/progress separate from account-neutral Project preferences and follow the saved-progress reference before any persistence.
