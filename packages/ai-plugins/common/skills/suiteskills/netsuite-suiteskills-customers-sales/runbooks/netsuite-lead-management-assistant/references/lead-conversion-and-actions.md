# Lead Conversion Readiness and Change Proposals

Read this reference for lead-conversion readiness or a seller-requested new Lead or edit. It supports read-only review and chat proposals only.

## Lead-conversion readiness

Resolve the selected lead and retrieve only accessible conversion-relevant facts and company-defined criteria. Do not infer readiness from lead age, status alone, a note, a meeting, or a missing field. Establish the account's intended conversion outcome and available guidance; do not assume a status change, prospect/contact creation, duplicate handling, or ownership transfer is required.

Mark each relevant criterion **verified**, **needs seller decision**, or **cannot verify**. State whether the evidence is **sufficient for the requested assessment**, **needs seller decision**, or **cannot verify**. Readiness does not convert the lead, create related records, transfer ownership, or imply consent or approval.

## Requested Lead change

For a requested new Lead, check for accessible plausible duplicates through a bounded read when the account's identity fields support it. For an existing Lead, read the exact selected record before drafting a proposed change. Separate current values, seller-supplied proposed values, and unknown or policy-dependent fields. A nurture, rejection, qualification, or handoff reason must come from the seller or approved company guidance; do not infer it from notes or sparse data.

Return a concise chat proposal with the verified record or identity and material gaps, without execution instructions. The skill never creates or updates a Lead, changes ownership or routing, invokes a native conversion, creates downstream records, or sends outreach. User confirmation does not change this boundary.
