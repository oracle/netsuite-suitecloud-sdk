# Lead Management Assistant changelog

## Included in SuiteSkills 1.0.0

Component version: 1.0.0. Source version: unknown.

Prioritize and qualify leads, prepare seller discovery and assess conversion or update readiness using read-only evidence. Use for lead queues, selected-lead context, qualification gaps and chat proposals. Excludes creating, updating, converting or contacting leads; campaign audience and performance work belongs to Marketing Campaign Assistant.
