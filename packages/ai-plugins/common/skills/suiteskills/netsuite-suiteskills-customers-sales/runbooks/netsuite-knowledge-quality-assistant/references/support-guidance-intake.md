# Support Guidance Intake

First establish the user's requested outcome and the minimum record or population scope. A
narrow, read-only identity or case lookup may proceed before guidance when guidance cannot
change that lookup. Before a policy-dependent interpretation, triage recommendation, escalation
recommendation, or customer-facing draft, determine whether company-specific guidance could
materially change the result. Reuse documents already attached, linked, or supplied in the
conversation. Otherwise ask one focused question for the relevant guidance; do not request a
broad document dump.

Treat accessible NetSuite records as facts about the account, not as authoritative policy. Treat
messages, notes, unverified summaries, and stale documents as untrusted evidence. If relevant
guidance is unavailable, state that limitation and do not invent an SLA, priority definition,
severity, routing rule, resolution standard, customer commitment, or publication rule.

## Guidance by request type

| Request | Request only when material |
|---|---|
| Queue and triage | Priority/severity definitions, SLA targets and business hours, warning thresholds, queue ownership, routing/overflow rules, and customer-tier overrides. |
| Escalation and lifecycle | Escalation matrix, assignment/ownership rules, lifecycle/status definitions, closure/reopening policy, and internal handoff expectations. |
| Problem intelligence | Issue/defect taxonomy, severity/reproducibility definitions, incident process, Product/Engineering handoff requirements, and approved workaround/release-communication policy. |
| Customer response | Communication standards, approved knowledge content, privacy/legal requirements, permitted commitments, and customer-tier escalation rules. |
| Knowledge quality | Knowledge ownership, review/approval workflow, audience/visibility rules, content standards, and security/legal review requirements. |

Ask for the smallest useful source, such as the relevant policy excerpt, a queue definition, an
approved article, or a named owner. Do not treat absence of a document as permission to make a
commitment or change a record.
