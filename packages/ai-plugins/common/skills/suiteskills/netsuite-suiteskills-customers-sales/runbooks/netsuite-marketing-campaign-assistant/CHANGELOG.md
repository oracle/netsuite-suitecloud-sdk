# Marketing Campaign Assistant changelog

Component version: 1.0.0. Source version: unknown.

Before querying campaign records, establish the relevant campaign capability and permission from current exposed evidence (LIST_CAMPAIGN is a candidate permission identifier, not an assumed universal flag).
