# Support evidence and audience

Load this reference for support-related requests when it adds useful context. It does not add a support-specific prerequisite to other runbooks. Exact fields, relationships and feature behavior come from current authorized sources, not this illustrative guidance.

- Resolve a displayed case number to the intended case's internal ID before dependent retrieval. Resolve named people according to the relevant customer/contact/employee relationship rather than assuming one entity type.
- Distinguish case fields, linked messages, notes, attachments and internal issue records. Record their actual relationship and source. A case header or message-like field does not by itself establish a complete conversation, authorship, original message or latest reply.
- Retrieve message content, attachments and audit details when relevant to the requested outcome. Start narrowly and expand when needed. There is no universal requirement to read every related message or to fetch all record fields.
- A zero-row or incomplete result describes the accessible retrieval path, not the absence of all notes, messages, attachments, defects or activity. Resolve material gaps through suitable supported alternatives.
- Keep customer-facing drafts appropriate to their audience. Do not disclose internal-only material without authorization. When visibility is unclear, flag that specific content for review and continue with information whose sharing is supported.
- Separate recorded status and timestamps from policy conclusions such as SLA breach, escalation eligibility, severity or promised resolution. Use [company guidance](COMPANY-GUIDANCE.md) when those conclusions depend on local rules.

Detailed schema recipes, audit anchors and platform timing claims belong in domain references with traceable documentation and account-validation context. Their absence here does not prevent research or a supported implementation. See [links](../contracts/LINKS.md) for useful case and related-record navigation.
